Profile

I hold an M.Sc. in GIS and Spatial Planning from the University of Porto. I have studied areas in Portugal, Spain, Norway, Brazil and the United States, and I now work on projects in Malaysia. My focus is Earth observation: Copernicus Sentinel and Landsat imagery, processed with Google Earth Engine and Python, to map floods, wildfires, land cover and urban heat. I am currently learning LiDAR to overcome the spectral limitation in my earlier work.

I am the first author of a peer-reviewed study on post-wildfire land cover change. At current role, I am the core analyst for a UNICEF programme and a team member on a heat map study for The Habitat Foundation. I also build pipelines, prototypes and web dashboards so that non-technical stakeholders can explore the findings themselves.

Land cover classification with Random Forest and SVM

Individual Serra da Estrela Natural Park, Portugal. Internship, University of Porto

Compared Random Forest and SVM, each with object-based and pixel-based approaches, on Sentinel-2A to classify land cover in the area burnt by the 2022 wildfire. Random Forest with OBIA scored highest on every metric, while SVM + PBIA resembled the NDVI and official COSc 2023 maps better.

Tools: Google Earth Engine, Sentinel-2, Python

100-year flood risk model

Group Study area: Aricanduva River Basin, São Paulo, Brazil. MSc project with Ashwin Kumar and Fernanda Costa Cavallaro

Delineated the watershed from a DEM, rebuilt 30 years of rainfall, applied Gumbel extreme-value analysis with Giandotti's method and simulated the 100-year flood with IBER 2D. The result matches the official municipal drainage studies.

Tools: ArcGIS Pro, IBER 2D, IDW, Thiessen polygons
